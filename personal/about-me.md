# Beyond the Code

## Hobbies & Side Projects

Outside of my day job I make art, and I've been building an online store with Medusa.js to actually sell it instead of letting it sit in a folder. It's the same instinct that drives most of my side projects: I don't like building throwaway demos, I'd rather build something real that solves a problem I actually have, even if the "problem" is just wanting a place to put my art.

I work out regularly to recharge — it's less about fitness goals and more about having a reset button for my brain after a day of debugging. I'm also currently teaching a friend how to program, which doubles as accountability for both of us to actually show up and train (coding and exercise both benefit from having someone else expecting you).

## Family & Language Learning

I've been studying Mandarin Chinese for over 10 years, going back to my Master's degree in Nanjing, China. After my daughter was born, I started an experiment: speaking to her only in Mandarin at home, to raise her bilingual. That turned into a real side project — a vocabulary app I built for myself because a Google Doc couldn't be searched mid-conversation with a toddler. It's on this site as the Mandarin vocab project.

Being a parent shapes a lot of how I work now: I'm more deliberate about time, and more drawn to projects with a real "why" behind them rather than portfolio filler.

## Personality & How I Work

I'm curious and a little restless — I tend to learn new things by building something with them rather than reading about them first, which is why side projects (the art store, the Mandarin app, this portfolio's RAG chat) exist in the first place. I'm disciplined about following through once I start, and I care about clean architecture and developer experience as much in a weekend project as I do at work.

Having lived and studied across Costa Rica and China, I'm used to adapting across languages and cultures, and that adaptability carries over into how I approach new stacks or teams — comfortable being the newest person in the room.
